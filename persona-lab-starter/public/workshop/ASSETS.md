# Prepared Persona Lab assets

Open `index.html` through the local dev server for the visual asset shelf.
Open `mock/index.html` for the connected pencil prototype.

## Source of truth

`content.json` supplies the brand, tagline, team roles, customer quotes,
supporters, and footer. Participants do not need to write a byline, upload
images, call an image service, or invent company information.

The pencil images show the layout and navigation, not final branding.
Their older names are superseded by `content.json`.

## Assets and themes

- `logos/persona-lab.svg`: editable speech-bubble/animal-face brand mark.
- `logos/`: fictional client and supporter wordmarks. Transparent backgrounds;
  edit SVG fill/stroke colors or frame them to suit your website theme.
- `characters/fox-ceo.png`: female fox CEO, glasses and orange scarf.
- `characters/codex-cto.png`: Codex robot CTO.
- `characters/dog-product.png`: dog Head of Product.
- The other seven character PNGs have transparent backgrounds.
- The three team portraits have cream backgrounds; do not assume transparency.

Keep the fictional-testimonial disclosure adjacent to the customer quotes.
Do not imply real customers, endorsements, investments, or measured outcomes.
Product findings are simulated hypotheses, not validated human research.

## Provenance

The character illustrations and pencil mock images were generated with OpenAI
image generation during this workshop's design exploration. Character designs
use the user-supplied Codex Masterclass Workshop Trail cast as reference.
The fox is female. The robot is the Codex workshop character, not a separate
company endorsement. Logos are authored SVG artwork for fictional companies.
No third-party portrait service or runtime image API is required.

This folder is prepared teaching material, not evidence of a real product.
